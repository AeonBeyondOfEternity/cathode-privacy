Account & Data Deletion

Delete Your Cathode Account

Cathode users may request deletion of their account and personal data associated with the application.

This page applies to Cathode, an independently developed Android application.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Cathode account, send an email to:

Braverisinc@gmail.com

Use the subject: Cathode Account Deletion

Please send the request using the email address associated with your Cathode account whenever possible. This helps us verify that the request relates to the correct account.

What Will Be Deleted

After the account and request have been appropriately verified, data associated with the Cathode account will be deleted where applicable.

This may include:

  • Cathode account-related records;
  • Firebase account information associated with Cathode;
  • Premium entitlement information associated with the account;
  • Temporary rewarded-ad unlock information associated with the account;
  • Other Cathode user data directly associated with the account.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when required for legitimate purposes such as:

  • Legal or regulatory obligations;
  • Fraud prevention;
  • Security;
  • Purchase verification;
  • Financial recordkeeping;
  • Dispute resolution.

Information processed independently by Google Play, Google AdMob, Firebase, or other Google services may also be subject to Google's own retention policies.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period and in accordance with applicable data protection law.

Contact

For questions concerning account or data deletion:

Braverisinc@gmail.com
