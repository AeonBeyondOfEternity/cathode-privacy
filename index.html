Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Introduction

Cathode is an independently developed Android application operated by an independent developer based in Brazil.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, processed, used, stored, and shared when you use Cathode.

By using Cathode, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Process

Cathode processes only information reasonably necessary to provide and maintain its features.

Depending on how you use the application, this may include:

  • Account information used to create and maintain your Cathode account.
  • Information associated with authentication, such as an email address, account identifier, or information provided through supported authentication services.
  • Information necessary to determine whether Premium features are available for your account.
  • Purchase and entitlement information necessary to verify and restore eligible purchases.
  • Technical information that may be automatically processed by the third-party services integrated into Cathode.

Guest Mode

Cathode may allow certain features to be used without creating or signing into a Cathode account.

When using Guest Mode, Cathode does not require you to provide account registration information for the basic functionality available to guests.

Some features may require authentication, including features where an account is necessary to securely associate or verify Premium entitlements or purchases.

Firebase

Cathode uses Google Firebase services to support account authentication and application database functionality.

Firebase may process account identifiers and other information necessary to authenticate users, maintain account-related information, and provide the application's backend functionality.

The specific information processed depends on the features you use and the authentication method selected.

Google Cloud and Backend Services

Cathode uses backend infrastructure hosted using Google Cloud services.

The backend may process account identifiers, purchase information, and entitlement information when necessary to securely verify purchases and determine whether an account has access to Premium features.

These systems are also used to help prevent unauthorized access to paid functionality and purchase-related abuse.

Advertising

Cathode uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers;
  • IP address;
  • Device and operating system information;
  • Advertising interactions;
  • Application interactions;
  • Diagnostic and performance information;
  • Other technical information necessary to provide and measure advertisements.

The exact information processed by Google may depend on your device, Google account, privacy settings, region, consent choices, and the advertising configuration used by Cathode.

In-App Purchases and Premium

Cathode may offer optional purchases that provide access to Premium features.

Payments are processed through Google Play using Google Play Billing.

Cathode does not directly receive or store your full credit card, debit card, or banking information.

Google Play handles payment processing.

Cathode and its backend may receive and process information relating to a transaction or purchase entitlement when necessary to verify the purchase, provide Premium access, prevent fraud, and restore eligible purchases.

How We Use Information

Information processed through Cathode may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain Cathode;
  • Create and authenticate user accounts;
  • Maintain account-related functionality;
  • Provide Guest Mode;
  • Verify purchases and Premium entitlements;
  • Restore eligible purchases;
  • Provide Premium features;
  • Display and manage advertisements;
  • Provide rewarded advertisements;
  • Protect paid functionality against unauthorized access;
  • Prevent fraud and abuse;
  • Maintain application and backend security;
  • Diagnose technical problems when applicable;
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing of Information

Cathode does not sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with service providers when necessary for them to provide services used by Cathode.

These service providers may include Google services used for:

  • Authentication;
  • Firebase database infrastructure;
  • Cloud backend infrastructure;
  • Advertising;
  • Payment processing;
  • Purchase verification;
  • Fraud prevention and security.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a valid request from a competent authority.
